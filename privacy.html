Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026. This page covers the "Talk to my AI" assistant on this site.

What the assistant is

An AI assistant that answers questions about Ahmed Tahoun's work and helps you book a call with him. It is not Ahmed, and it always says so.

What is collected

  • What you say or type in the conversation, and the details you choose to share (for example your name, email, company and what you're looking for).
  • For voice chats, the audio of the conversation, processed by ElevenLabs to understand and answer you.
  • If you book a call, the booking details you enter in the calendar (handled by Cal.com).

Nothing is collected until you start a conversation. Browsing the rest of the site does not send your data anywhere. The site remembers your language and theme in your own browser only.

Why

Only so Ahmed can reply to you, follow up on a job or project, and improve the assistant. Your details are never sold or used for advertising.

Who handles it

  • ElevenLabs runs the voice and chat conversation.
  • Cal.com handles bookings.
  • A summary is sent to Ahmed through Telegram and kept on this site's server.

How long it's kept

Conversation notes are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Your choices

You can use the site without the assistant, or type instead of using your microphone. To see or delete what you shared, email ahmed.tahoun.at@gmail.com and it will be removed.
